# FUTUREVIEW Personal — Design Direction

FUTUREVIEW is a creative, interactive, clean simulation playground. The spatial scene dominates: users should see rooms, objects, doors, agents, paths, and consequences before they see analysis.

Use a restrained dark visual system with thin borders, low-radius surfaces, clear hierarchy, and minimal decorative effects. Color communicates state—such as movement, blocked paths, density, or comparison—not branding noise. Avoid neon/cyberpunk styling, glassmorphism-heavy surfaces, AI-generated decoration, and metric-card-heavy dashboards.

Interactions should feel direct: place, select, move, open/close, run, pause, compare. Results use progressive disclosure: state the outcome in human language first; expose Why/Details next; expose deeper metrics only on request. Keep controls present but visually subordinate to the scene.
